About Us

how two weirdos became one soul 💕

The first day I came to hostel, there was just a bed. Literally just a bed. I felt so weird about it — like, this is it? But then you walked in, and something just clicked. I never imagined I'd become this close to you.

Okay, let's be honest — you're not that great. 😏 But somehow you're still the best? We became so close so fast, had so many misunderstandings, and there are SO many things you do that I don't like — but hey, not everyone can be perfect like me. 💅

And whenever I cried over the stupidest things — you were there. Every single time. You just showed up, no questions asked.

And then there's the day you got me on the wrong train. You literally just took me somewhere random. Iconic. 🚃

Now I've gotten so close to you that whenever you're not there, I genuinely don't know what to do with myself. And when you leave for your internship? I think I'll actually die. So before that, I also need to find one — because I refuse to be left behind sobbing in the hostel room. 😭
