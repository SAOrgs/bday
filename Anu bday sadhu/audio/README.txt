HOW TO ADD BACKGROUND MUSIC
===========================

1. Put your song in THIS folder and name it:  song.mp3
   (so the full path is  audio/song.mp3 )

2. That's it. The little music button in the bottom-right corner of every page
   will play/pause it, and the on/off choice is remembered as she clicks between
   pages.

NOTES
-----
- Web browsers block music from auto-starting until the visitor clicks something,
  so the song begins the first time she taps the music button. This is normal and
  can't be avoided.
- To use a different filename, change this line in js/main.js:

      audio.src = 'audio/song.mp3';

- .mp3 works everywhere. .ogg / .m4a also work in most browsers.
- Pick something soft and sweet 💕
